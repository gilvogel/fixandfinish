01 / TILE & GROUT

Tile & Grout Service

A focused repair can refresh your tile without replacing the whole room. We take care of damaged tiles, worn grout, and failing caulk to help your kitchen or bathroom look cared for again.

  • Replace cracked or loose tiles
  • Repair damaged grout and caulking
  • Re-caulk tubs and showers
  • Repair small areas of shower walls and floors
  • Replace damaged backsplash tiles

02 / SMALL PLUMBING REPAIRS

Small Plumbing Repairs

Get help with the everyday fixture problems that interrupt your routine, from a running toilet to a dripping faucet. We discuss the repair and confirm the work is within our qualifications before scheduling.

  • Replace faucets and showerheads
  • Repair running toilets and replace fill valves
